# Farming

**Which farm plant pays best?** The {{best}}: about {{bestDay}} units a plant a day, sold at a space station. NipNip Buds sell for {{nipStationLow}} to {{nipStationHigh}} times their value, but they grow slower.

## The plants

A base grows {{plants}} farm plants. Each gives a fixed harvest when it is ripe, and the harvest sells at a trade terminal. The table runs by what a plant earns in a day of growing, harvested as soon as it ripens.

<!-- widget:plants -->

Every plant but the Echinocactus needs the [Haz-Mat Gauntlet](/guides/exosuit-modules#every-exosuit-module) to harvest, an exosuit module you build. The plants that give a stack of a resource (Frost Crystal, Star Bulb, Fungal Mould, Gamma Root, Faecium, Mordite, Solanium, Cactus Flesh) are worth little to sell; they are for crafting and refining.

## How much to plant

Gathering is a hold of the interact key, {{harvestSeconds}} of a second a plant, so an hour of holding picks about {{plantsAnHour}} plants; the step to the next plant comes on top. Plant no more than you can gather before the first ones ripen again: a Gravitino Host ripens in {{gravitinoGrowth}}, so about {{gravitinoPlants}} plants fill one round, worth about {{gravitinoCrop}} units at full price. NipNip ripens in {{nipnipGrowth}}: {{nipnipPlants}} plants, about {{nipnipCrop}} units. A crop that big sells at full price only spread over many stations, as below, and selling takes time on top of that.

## Where to sell

A space station pays more than a terminal on a planet or a freighter. Every system draws its own price, so the same crop fetches a little more in one system than in the next.

Selling lowers the price of what you sell, at that terminal only. For a product such as Gravitino Balls, every {{productUnits}} units sold take {{productDrop}} off the multiplier; for a stacked resource, every {{substanceUnits}} units take {{substanceDrop}}. The price comes back slowly: one unit's worth a minute for a product. So a big crop sells best split over several stations.

NipNip Buds are contraband. Outside a pirate system they sell with the smuggler's markup, {{smuggler}} times: {{nipStationLow}} to {{nipStationHigh}} times their value at a space station, {{nipTerminalLow}} to {{nipTerminalHigh}} times at a planet terminal. In a pirate system they sell near their value.

## A crop against a crashed Interceptor

A claimed Sentinel Interceptor at class C sells for {{interceptorLow}} to {{interceptorHigh}} units, by its cargo slots, as [Interceptor prices](/guides/interceptor-prices) shows. One claimed Interceptor sells for as much as a hundred to several hundred Gravitino Host harvests, so farming pays for those who build a big farm and sell over many stations; [Sentinel Interceptors](/guides/interceptors) says where the wrecks lie.

What a crop is worth more as, once it is cooked, is on [best-selling dishes](/guides/best-selling-dishes).
