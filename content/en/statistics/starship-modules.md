# Starship modules

**What can a starship upgrade module raise?** Each system of a starship has its own family of upgrade modules, and the class sets how many stats one install raises and how far: an S-Class Pulse Engine upgrade raises three of its stats, a C-Class one raises one or two. The starship merchant of a station sells them, as [station technology merchants](/guides/technology-merchants) shows.

Each stat reads as the module's card prints it. Illegal modules are sold at an [Outlaw Station](/guides/outlaw-station) and now and then by the [Scrap Dealer](/guides/scrap-dealer), and Rusted ones are sea salvage.

## Upgrade modules

One table per family: each class, how many stats one install raises, and the limits of each stat. Below each table a second one shows what one install rolls: how many stats it raises, how often it comes near the best its kind can add to the stat it names, and how often each stat is raised; a third shows how often a raised stat lands in the top tenth of its limits, where the Illegal modules stand out by a one-in-ten draw that raises the most stats their kind allows. Where to put them for the most damage is on [supercharged slots](/guides/supercharged-slots).

Under the three tables of a family stands what to take: how an A-Class or an Illegal install compares with an S-Class one in what the family is for, as often as it adds at least what a middling S-Class install adds, more than the best one, or less than the worst.

### Hyperdrive

<!-- widget:hdriveTop -->

For range, class is a wall below S: no A-Class install reaches the worst S-Class one. Above S stands only the Illegal module, which can jump further than any S-Class one, {{hdriveXOverBestS}} of the time; but an Illegal install does not always raise range at all, as the second table shows, and {{hdriveXUnderWorstS}} end up below the worst S-Class install. Three S-Class modules are the sure way; swap one for an Illegal only when its card beats the S you hold.

The colour drives are not upgrade modules. A Cadmium, Emeril, or Indium Drive lets the hyperdrive reach red, green, or blue stars, and each is a blueprint of the Space Anomaly; they are in the table of every starship module. The Atlantid Drive, which reaches the purple stars, is a blueprint of the Space Anomaly too, and the quest [In Stellar Multitudes](/quests/in-stellar-multitudes) gives it.

### Pulse Engine

The Pulse Engine is the family where class matters least for Boost: {{shipjumpAAtTypicalS}} of A-Class installs and {{shipjumpXAtTypicalS}} of Illegal ones match a middling S-Class one, and an Illegal install beats the best S-Class one {{shipjumpXOverBestS}} of the time. What S-Class buys is certainty: it always raises all three stats, and the others do not.

### Launch Thruster

<!-- widget:launcher -->

<!-- widget:launcherRolls -->

<!-- widget:launcherTop -->

An S-Class Launch Thruster module always cuts the launch cost by the same amount, so there is nothing to roll for: buy three and you are done. An Illegal one cuts more {{launcherXOverBestS}} of the time and less the rest, and no A-Class install matches S.

### Deflector Shield

<!-- widget:shipshld -->

<!-- widget:shipshldRolls -->

<!-- widget:shipshldTop -->

The shield works like the Launch Thruster: every S-Class install adds the same Shield Strength, and no A-Class one reaches it. Only an Illegal install goes higher, {{shipshldXOverBestS}} of the time; the other {{shipshldXUnderWorstS}} are weaker than S. Three S-Class modules are the sure shield; the Illegal draw is for the last few percent.

### Photon Cannon

<!-- widget:shipgun -->

<!-- widget:shipgunRolls -->

<!-- widget:shipgunTop -->

An A-Class Photon Cannon module is rarely worth a slot: {{shipgunAUnderWorstS}} of its installs add less damage per second than the worst S-Class one. Illegal installs split the same way as elsewhere, {{shipgunXOverBestS}} above the best S-Class install and {{shipgunXUnderWorstS}} below the worst. These are the modules that also raise an [Interceptor](/guides/interceptors)'s Sentinel Cannon.

### Phase Beam

<!-- widget:shiplas -->

<!-- widget:shiplasRolls -->

<!-- widget:shiplasTop -->

The Phase Beam's classes do not overlap: no A-Class install reaches the worst S-Class one in Damage. An Illegal install beats the best S-Class one {{shiplasXOverBestS}} of the time, and {{shiplasXUnderWorstS}} fall below the worst, partly because an Illegal module does not always raise Damage.

### Positron Ejector

<!-- widget:shipshot -->

<!-- widget:shipshotRolls -->

<!-- widget:shipshotTop -->

For the Positron Ejector, S-Class is the top: its Damage is one fixed value, no Illegal install beats it, and no A-Class install reaches it. An Illegal module has no edge here at all: its Fire Rate and Heat Dispersion reach no higher than S-Class's, and often lower.

### Infra-Knife Accelerator

<!-- widget:shipmini -->

<!-- widget:shipminiRolls -->

<!-- widget:shipminiTop -->

In Damage the Infra-Knife's classes sit close: {{shipminiAAtTypicalS}} of A-Class installs match a middling S-Class one, and {{shipminiXOverBestS}} of Illegal ones beat the best. What sets S-Class apart is Heat Dispersion, where its limits start where A-Class's end, so S keeps the gun firing longer.

### Cyclotron Ballista

<!-- widget:shipblob -->

<!-- widget:shipblobRolls -->

<!-- widget:shipblobTop -->

The Cyclotron Ballista gains almost nothing in Damage from any class, so judge its modules by Fire Rate and Heat Dispersion: there S-Class starts where A-Class ends, and only an Illegal install can go past it, when it raises those stats at all.

## Every starship module

The modules you build or receive, apart from upgrade modules and the cosmetics. How to upgrade the ship itself to class S is on [how to upgrade a ship](/guides/ship-upgrades). The [Base Computer Archives](/quests/base-computer-archives) of your base give the Teleport Receiver and the Efficient Thrusters, and the armourer's quest [Weapons Research](/quests/weapons-research) the Positron Ejector, the Infra-Knife Accelerator, and the Cyclotron Ballista.

### Interceptor only

A Sentinel Interceptor flies on its own core technology, and no other ship takes it; the [Interceptors](/guides/interceptors) guide says where to find one.

<!-- widget:onlyInterceptor -->

### Living ship only

A [living ship](/guides/living-ships) grows organs in the place of a starship's technology: none of these fits another ship, and of the starship modules it takes only the figurines, the trails, and the Atlantid Drive.

<!-- widget:onlyLiving -->

## Figurines

A figurine sits in the cockpit, takes a technology slot, and changes a stat. Six are sold for Quicksilver, the rest are expedition rewards.

<!-- widget:figurines -->

## Starship trails

A trail colours the exhaust and adds 1% to boost. It is not a drive: the Cadmium or Emeril Drive in the list of every starship module opens stars of that colour, a trail of the same name only paints the exhaust. Five trails are sold for Quicksilver, the rest are expedition rewards.

<!-- widget:trails -->

A corvette you build yourself takes these modules, and each part it is built from brings one more, as [corvettes](/guides/corvettes) shows and [corvette modules](/guides/corvette-modules) lists.
