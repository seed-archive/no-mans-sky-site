# Charts

**Where do you get a chart, and what does each one find?** Most come from the [Cartographer](/guides/cartographer) of a space station for Navigation Data; the rest from a [Deep-Space Outpost](/guides/deep-space-outpost), the [Scrap Dealer](/guides/scrap-dealer), Sentinels, the Holo-Arena, and the crafting menu. Each marks one place on your map.

## Every chart

<!-- widget:charts -->

A chart searches the planet or system you are in, and most mark the nearest place of their kind. A Settlement Chart picks any settlement of the planet, and the Habitable Asteroid Chart and the Champion's Invitation may lead to a near system.

## What a Planetary Chart marks

The Cartographer sells four sorts of Planetary Chart. Each marks one building, drawn by the chances below, so pick the sort by what you are after: an Emergency chart for a crashed freighter or a distress signal, a Commercial one for a shop or a dig site, an Alien one for a monolith or a ruin, a Secret one for a depot, a harvester or a factory.
