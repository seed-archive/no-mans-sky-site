## How to make them

Each of these dishes, and the Anomalous Doughnut, is shown made from what you gather: every step through the Nutrient Processor and the refiners, the cheapest way. Short shows the last step alone.

<!-- widget:made -->

No dish goes past {{ceiling}}. A dish's bonus is fixed: the same dish gives the same bonus every time you cook it, so the table is all you need to pick one. The same hunt for a ceiling, among creatures, is on [best creatures](/guides/best-creatures).

## How the bonus works

Put the dish into the slot of the Nutrient Ingestor, one of the [exosuit modules](/guides/exosuit-modules), and it eats one unit at a time until the stack runs out; the menu shows the bonus and how long the stack lasts. Only one dish works at a time, so two bonuses never add up. Without the module there is no bonus: eaten by hand, a dish gives a short effect of its own and nothing more.

The bonus does not add to your Analysis Visor's reward: it multiplies it. The visor and every upgrade module of that reward are each multiplied by the bonus read as a multiplier, so the better your modules, the more the dish is worth.
