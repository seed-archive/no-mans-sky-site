# Planet globe

**What does a planet of the catalog look like, and where on it is each find?** Pick a planet and turn it: the globe shows its land and seas in the planet's own colours, its mountains, and a dot on every building we list there. A gold dot holds a multitool or a crashed ship; click it to open the find.

<!-- widget:finder -->

## How to use it

Drag to turn the planet and use the wheel to come closer. The list holds every planet of the catalog; a planet's own page links here too, with the planet already picked.

The ground takes the colours the game gives the planet, and the mountains are shaded from one side so their shape shows. The globe is drawn by the archive, not taken from the game, so a coastline or a colour may differ a little from what you see in orbit.
