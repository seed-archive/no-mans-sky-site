# Expeditions

**What do expeditions give, and can you still get it?** Each of the {{expeditions}} expeditions unlocks its rewards on your account, {{seasonRewards}} in all, {{expeditionShips}} starships among them, and every save you play collects them at the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion of the Space Anomaly.

## Rewards that carry to every save

An expedition is a save of its own, a race through set milestones from a set start. What you unlock there belongs to your account: the game says its exclusive rewards "can be accessed on any save". Open the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion on the Space Anomaly and choose Collect expedition rewards; each save collects each reward once. Twitch drops come the same way, through Collect Twitch rewards, once your Hello Games account is linked. A reward you decline inside an expedition stays open for your other saves; one you decline for good is gone.

Rewards of an edition or a platform come from the options, under Redeem Bonus Content, out of your ship and with two free inventory slots.

## 1. The Pioneers

*"Blaze a trail as one of the first of a new group of pioneers."* Spring 2021, rerun that winter. The first expedition: every player began on one planet with a C-class explorer and a stronger start, on a plain road through the game. It gave the Golden Vector, and its starship trails come from no other source.

## 2. Beachhead

*"Delve into the mystery of a starship from another universe..."* May 2021. A Mass Effect crossover that opened with a freighter already yours; its prize, the SSV Normandy SR1, came back with the 2025 reruns.

## 3. The Cartographers

*"Explore a planet in detail as you struggle to escape its surface."* Autumn 2021. The whole run on one toxic planet, rebuilding a broken exotic starship. It gave the Whispering Companion Egg.

## 4. Emergence

*"Face the titan worm and its many-mouthed vessels."* From October 2021. A start on an extreme barren world among the Titan Worms, the phases named as hunts of the worm. Only it gave the Visceral Synthesiser, the Archive Bypass Implant, and the Ghastly Trail; its reward is the Sandworm Egg.

## 5. Exobiology

*"Befriend, nurture, and interact with the creatures of the universe in a gentle journey of discovery."* Early 2022, rerun that winter. Taming, breeding, eggs, milk, and baking, from a frozen start with a companion. It taught the Deep-Space Lure, one of the [charts](/guides/charts), and gave the Quad Egg.

## 6. The Blighted

*"Hunt down a rampaging pirate faction and uncover their secret treasure."* Spring 2022, rerun that winter. A pirate treasure trail, crafted map by map from three Pirate Map Fragments, with no base building until late. It gave the Void Egg.

## 7. Leviathan

*"Death is not the end; the loop continues."* Summer 2022. A roguelike time loop: death restarted the save, progress carried between loops, and community research gave upgrades. Its end meets the Leviathan, a creature of space. It taught the Memory Fragment, the Memory Resonator, and the Whalesong Flute, and gave the Organic Frigate; the Solar Ray and three exocraft suspensions came from it too.

## 8. Polestar

*"Cruise across the galaxy on an interstellar freighter voyage."* Summer 2022. The freighter was the way to travel, the starship had no hyperdrive. It taught the Homeworld Repeater, gave the Helios Egg, and alone the Singularity Engine.

## 9. Utopia

*"The Utopia Foundation welcomes all contributors to our ambitious new communal habitation project."* From February 2023. Crafting only inside a base, a ship that could not be repaired at first, travel by exocraft, and the planets of one system restored. It gave the Utopia Speeder and the Robo-Warden Egg.

## 10. Singularity

*"Solve a mystery that echoes deep within the fabric of reality."* From June 2023. A story told in pilgrimages between harmonic camps, rebuilding a Construct part by part: its head, legs, limbs, and shell, and the five Seeds that wake it, are its recipes.

## 11. Voyagers

*"Explore the universe and catalogue its marvels."* From August 2023. An exploring catalogue with no hazard of its own. It gave the Construct 'Egg'.

## 12. Omega

*"Begin a journey that has no end, on an infinite road into the stars."* Early 2024. The first expedition you could play from your main save, through the Space Anomaly, from a start under a derelict freighter. It taught the four Memories and gave the Starborn Runner and the Atlas Sceptre.

## 13. Adrift

*"Alone in an empty universe..."* Summer 2024. A universe without people or other life, and technology broken from the start. It gave the Iron Vulture, the cursed frigate Ship of the Damned, and the Appalling Egg.

## 14. Liquidators

*"The vile brood spread their corruption across the universe..."* Summer 2024. A combat run through infested dropzones with a Sentinel multitool. Only it gave the Liquidator body, arms, and legs of the Minotaur, and the Grub Cocoon.

## 15. Aquarius

*"Join the Aquarius Expedition to catch fish..."* From September 2024. Fishing, with a bait for each habitat and the Exo-Skiff; only it gave the Lost Angler's Rig.

## 16. The Cursed

*"...travel beyond the boundaries and cross over into a universe of haunting anomalies..."* October 2024, two weeks. The planets' hazards gave way to one, the boundary, and travel went by portal alone. Its end is the Boundary Guardian, called up with the Elixir of Water; the four Elixirs are its recipes. It gave the Boundary Herald and the Glowing Egg. The boundary and its horrors came back with the [Forsaken Trader](/guides/forsaken-trader).

## 17. Titan

*"Join the Titan Expedition on a voyage through a dazzling array of new worlds and new stars..."* From February 2025. The new worlds of Worlds Part II. It gave The Wraith and the staff Pillar of Titan.

## 18. Relics

*"Join the Relics Expedition to unearth ancient treasures and recreate the lost beasts of the past..."* Spring 2025, rerun that December. Fossils, rebuilt creatures, and ancient guardians at dig sites. It gave the Fossilised Egg and the staff Basilisk Crown.

## 19. Corvette

*"Design, assemble and upgrade a habitable starship..."* Autumn 2025. No starship trade: you built a Corvette under Sentinel pressure, reaching the station through the Orbital Uplink, its one recipe. It gave the Mecha-Mouse Egg.

## 20. Breach

*"Sail through the stars, confronting both the awe and the horror that lurks in deep space..."* From October 2025. Deep-space horror among derelicts, ending at Jellyflesh, the Deepwater Guardian.

## 21. Remnant

*"Sign up for heavy-duty industrial salvage!"* February 2026. No starship at all: one desert planet, the Colossus, and waste hauled with the Gravitino Coil. It gave Colossus parts.

## 22. Swarm

*"A new threat has surfaced. The Traveller soul is fragmented, and something terrible hangs in the sky..."* From May 2026. Three teams, Royal, Sage, and Weaver, chosen by a quiz, building the Prismatic Core against the Hive of Glass; the echoes of each team are its recipes. Only the winning team kept the core. It gave the Direwasp Disintegrator.

## 23. Our Journey Continues

*"Travel through iterations of an infinite universe on a special anniversary expedition."* September 2026, the game's tenth anniversary: each milestone one update of the game, with the developers' commentary. It taught the Like-Seed and the Myth Beacon and gave the Golden Rasamama S36, the Vintage Interceptor, the Starbound v0.27, and the Diplo Egg.
